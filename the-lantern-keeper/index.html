From the first chapter

The light was not for ships

The first lantern was not for ships. Edith Voss had been told that by her grandmother, and Grayharbor had spent forty years pretending not to hear. On clear nights the beam still went out, because a town that only lights itself in a storm starts to believe it is braver than it is.
