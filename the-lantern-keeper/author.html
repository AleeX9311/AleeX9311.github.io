About the author

Mara Ellison

Mara Ellison is a novelist who does not exist, which keeps her calendar wonderfully clear. She was invented for this sample site. She is not based on a living person.

In the fiction, she writes before the house wakes and keeps a pencil in the book she is reading so the place does not get lost. The Lantern Keeper is her first novel. She claims Grayharbor is nowhere in particular, then describes the diner with suspicious accuracy.

  • Lives where the map goes blank, on purpose.
  • Writes one page before breakfast.
  • Will answer a letter. Will not answer a trend.
