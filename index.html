Process

How a project moves

Four steps. You always know which one we are in.

  1. 01

    Brief

    You tell me who the site is for and what it has to do. A short email is enough to start.

  2. 02

    First version

    A working first version in 2–3 days, so we look at a real page instead of a long proposal.

  3. 03

    Revisions

    Revisions are included. We change the words, the layout, and the details.

  4. 04

    Launch

    I put the site on your hosting, or I hand you the files. You keep them.
